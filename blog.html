Starting GDG on Campus TU Kenya from zero

In April 2026 there was no GDG chapter at TU Kenya — just an idea and a 90-day roadmap. I ran an info session, recorded it, submitted the wrapup, and started building a core team from scratch. We're at 149 members now, up from zero. The hard part was never getting people to join — it was getting people to stay active. I've had to learn how to recruit, how to hold people accountable without being a pain about it, and how to actually remove someone from a team without burning the relationship.

Running an event on a near-zero budget

Most of our events draw 2–7 people. That's humbling when you're also trying to plan a Hacktoberfest hackathon with Major League Hacking. I've had to split chapter funds carefully between this semester and next, keep things transparent with the team, and figure out what's actually worth spending on versus what's just ambition outpacing turnout.

About me

I'm a second-year IT student at the Technical University of Kenya, splitting my time between two things that don't usually end up on the same page: offensive security and building community around tech in Kenya.

I run a homelab at vulnbox.co.ke where I set up vulnerable targets and break them myself, because that's the only way I actually learn how something fails. Most of my week outside class goes into CTFs — OSINT is my lane, but I'm pushing further into reverse engineering and binary exploitation. eJPT is the next checkpoint, OSCP is the long game.

I also founded GDG on Campus TU Kenya from nothing back in April 2026 — no existing chapter, no playbook, just an info session and a 90-day roadmap. It's grown to 149 members since. Running it taught me as much about people and follow-through as any CTF has taught me about machines.

I decided a while back to go deep on security first, before chasing a startup or a job. Everything else — the website, the hiking, the fanpage throwback — waits its turn around that.

Outside of all this: basketball, anime, hiking with the Milima na Marafiki crew, and reading whatever Harold Robbins or Sidney Sheldon paperback I can find — which nobody guesses from the rest of this page. I care a lot about people being treated fairly, and I don't pretend to be unemotional about things that matter to me.
